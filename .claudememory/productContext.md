# Product Context

## Purpose
- **Retention: AI Flashcards** is a brutally minimalist study system for students and self-learners: flashcards, fill-in-the-blank, and practice quizzes, with AI that turns PDFs or text into decks. Works offline, syncs through the user's own Google Drive.
- Owner/author: Tony. Published on the Chrome Web Store; web app on GitHub Pages; installable as a phone PWA.

## User Workflows
- **Capture:** highlight text on any page, right-click "Add to Retention (AI)" / "Make practice quiz (AI)" (extension).
- **Generate:** drag a PDF (or pick one from Google Drive, or paste notes) and let AI write flashcards or a quiz; needs the user's OpenRouter API key.
- **Organize:** nested folders, drag-and-drop, "Move to...", CSV import/export.
- **Study:** flip cards and mark Know / Skip / Forgot (mastery %); type answers on fill-in-the-blank cards; session resumes where you left off; folders can be studied as one shuffled session.
- **Practice quizzes:** multiple choice, true/false, fill in the blank; answers shown right away or at the end; scores tracked separately.
- **Personalize:** 21+ themes (Monochrome, Retro Tech, Paper & Print, Cozy & Earthy, Cafe Treats, Celestial, Landscapes, Star Wars), favorites with a quick menu.
- **Sync:** Google Drive auto-sync across extension, web, and phone.
